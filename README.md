# Apocalypse Sales Dashboard

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing survival product sales across four prepper stores. The dashboard tracks units sold, revenue, production cost, customer purchasing behavior, and state-level distribution.

## Tools
- Power BI
- Excel
- DAX

## Dataset
- `sales_data.xlsx`
- Tables: Apocolypse Store, Apocolypse Sales, Customer Information, Customer Buyer Information
- Time range: January 2022 – March 2022
- Records: 75 sales transactions, 50 buyer records, 10 products, 4 customers

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards: Count of Total Purchased, Min of Total Purchased, Sum of Total Purchased
- Sum of Units Sold by Product Name
- Price and Production Cost by Product Name
- Total Purchased by State (donut chart)
- Customer Purchase Breakdown (100% stacked bar)
- Products Purchased by Date (trend line)

## Key Insights
- Multitool Survival Knife (477), Nylon Rope (390), and Duct Tape (365) are the top-selling products by units.
- Uncle Joe's Prep Shop is the highest-volume customer with 951 units purchased, followed by Apocolypse Preppers United (828).
- MN (28.14%), DE (17.57%), and NC (12.41%) together account for over 58% of total units purchased.
- Customer purchase patterns vary by store: Prep4Anything buys mostly N95 Masks (39.57%), while Apocolypse Preppers United buys mostly Multitool Survival Knives (26.69%).
- Sales peaked in January–February 2022 and declined in March.
- Multitool Survival Knife has a high gross margin (~63%) and high sales volume, making it a core profit product.
- Duct Tape has high sales but low margin (~22%), suggesting it works as a traffic driver rather than a profit driver.

## Business Recommendations
- Prioritize inventory for Multitool Survival Knife, Nylon Rope, and Duct Tape.
- Focus retention efforts on Uncle Joe's Prep Shop.
- Target marketing and distribution in MN, DE, and NC.
- Tailor product recommendations by customer segment.
- Investigate the March sales decline to determine whether it is seasonal or data-related.
- Review pricing or cost structure for low-margin products such as Duct Tape.
